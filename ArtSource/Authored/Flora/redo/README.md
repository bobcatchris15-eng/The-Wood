# redo batch (authored)

Generated by `Tools/Author-Flora-redo.py` (headless Blender 5.2). Deterministic: same seed and parameters give the same geometry.

Redo of rejected items: baldcypress (+ knees prop), lady_fern, alpine_tussock, shade_grass, horsetail, bracket_fungus, duckweed_mat, arctic_poppy, lupine. Each variant is one combined mesh with several material slots.

Conventions: metres. Blender scene Z-up. GLB is Y-up (glTF spec). FBX is Blender-native Z-up. Origin at ground centre (min Z = 0). Exceptions: `bracket_fungus` origin is on the attach face (back plane y = 0, shelves grow toward +Y, lowest tier at z = 0); `duckweed_mat` is a thin 1 m plane at z = 3 mm.

Vertex colour `WindWeight` (COLOR_0 in GLB): R = wind weight (height ratio; radial for the tree), G = B = 0, A = 1. Fungus, duckweed and knees are all 0.

Foliage materials are alpha-masked (MASK, cutoff 0.5) and two-sided. LOD1 (about 30% tris, regrown at lower density) is written only where LOD0 exceeds 2000 triangles.

Previews: `_preview.png` 3/4 view, `_near.png` close view (baldcypress: eye height 5 m from the crown, plus `_far.png` at 30 m), bracket also `_top.png`. Cov = opaque fraction inside the convex hull of the silhouette (side view).

## Structure notes

- baldcypress: narrow pyramid of whorled drooping branches carrying overlapping feathery needle sprays (three planes) plus hanging curtains; trunk has a lobed buttress flare. Knees are a separate prop (`baldcypress_knees_vNN`).
- lady_fern: 15-19 bipinnate fronds in a vase; frond texture drawn as pinnae divided into separated pinnules (lacy, gaps).
- alpine_tussock / shade_grass: 300-400 tapered blades per tuft arched into a dense dome; blade atlas tones.
- horsetail: 11-13 jointed stems (collared nodes with dark bands) with whorls of fine needle branches.
- bracket_fungus: flat semicircular shelves with concentric zoned bands (procedural), pale margin, pore underside, 3-5 stacked tiers.
- duckweed_mat: single plane, irregular tiny ovals in clumps with open gaps, alpha-masked.
- arctic_poppy: cup-shaped 4-5 petal blooms (yellow v01, white v02, orange v03) on hairy stems over a lobed leaf rosette.
- lupine: palmate basal leaves, dense conical spikes of pea-flower cards (blue v01, violet v02, pink v03).
